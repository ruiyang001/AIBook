# Eva and the Digital Parrot

An interactive digital children's book about AI literacy, designed for children to read together with caregivers or teachers. Its illustrated pages invite conversations about AI through a familiar, animated page-turning experience.

**[Read the book online](https://ruiyang001.github.io/aibook/)**

## Reading controls

- Click a page corner, or drag/swipe from a page edge to turn pages.
- Use the previous/next arrows to move backward or forward.
- On desktop, use the keyboard's left and right arrow keys.
- Use the slider below the book to jump through the story and preview its illustrations.

The reader uses two-page spreads on larger screens and a single-page view on smaller screens.
