About This Book
This book is a guide for caregivers and teachers to use during co-reading sessions to discuss AI technology. It is not intended for children to read alone, as reading together allows adults to facilitate discussions while the story unfolds.
The story introduces the core concept of pattern matching: the way AI mimics language without true understanding. It explains that while AI can use words about feelings, it lacks real emotions. By comparing AI to a complex mimicking machine, the book helps children distinguish between having information and being alive. This framework prepares young readers to understand how digital assistants process the world.
For adults, this book can also serve as a tool to address anthropomorphism. Children often project human traits onto digital assistants. Through simple experiments, the story helps children build critical thinking. Eva moves from relying on Artie for answers to using it as a creative partner. It is a journey of reconciliation and growth, where the child learns to be the Captain of their imagination.
